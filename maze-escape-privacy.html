Privacy Policy

Maze Escape: Slide Puzzle

Last updated: October 4, 2026

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

Bright Orbit Games develops Maze Escape: Slide Puzzle for Android, identified by the package com.brightorbitgames.mazeescape. This policy describes how the game and its ads handle data. For privacy questions, write to admin@brightorbitgames.com.

The game does not require you to create an account or sign in. It does not include chat between players or its own cloud sync of your progress.

2. Progress, settings and local statistics

The game stores on your device your campaign progress, lives, unlocked shapes and skins, records, the state of hints and tutorials, and your music, sound and vibration preferences.

On Android you can also indicate an age range: under 13, 13–17, or 18 and over, or choose not to say. No date of birth is requested. Your selection is kept on this device to configure how ads are handled; Google receives the corresponding restriction tags, not a date of birth or an exact age.

The game also keeps local counters of games and attempts, moves, play time, completed levels, losses, restarts, hints and interruptions, along with a reference to the day of your last session. They are used to preserve your game experience and to allow diagnosis of how the game is working.

These records are not automatically sent to Bright Orbit Games or to any statistics server of our own. Local diagnostics do not include names, email addresses, device identifiers or location. This does not describe the data that Google AdMob may process, which is explained separately.

3. Diagnostics you choose to share

If you use “Copy diagnostics” (“Copiar diagnóstico” in the Spanish version) in the settings, the game copies to your clipboard a report of its counters and overall progress. It does not send it on its own. You decide whether to paste it and share it with us or anyone else. The report does not include your age range, the date of your last session or a detailed event history.

4. Google AdMob ads

The game integrates Google AdMob to offer rewarded ads, for example to get a hint or continue a game. Watching them is optional.

The advertising service may collect and share:

  • Your IP address and the approximate location that may be derived from it.
  • Advertising identifiers and other device or app-set identifiers, when available.
  • Interactions, such as ad launches, taps and views.
  • Technical performance and diagnostic information about the app and the advertising service.

Google processes this information to serve and measure ads, perform analytics and prevent fraud. Consent preferences and the service configuration determine how ads are processed and personalized.

You can read the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information for advertising.

5. Consent and controls

Ad processing must respect the consent and restrictions that apply under applicable law and the service configuration. Declining personalization does not delete your locally saved progress. Non-personalized ads may also require processing technical data.

You can access this policy from Settings. When Google’s consent service requires it, a “Privacy options” item (“Opciones de privacidad” in the Spanish version) appears so you can reopen the form and review or withdraw your choices. The game checks the service’s permission before loading ads and discards previously loaded ads when you review these options.

“Age range” (“Rango de edad”) lets you correct your selection or withdraw it with “Prefer not to say” (“Prefiero no indicarlo”). If it changes after the advertising component has been created, ads are disabled until you close and reopen the game. This allows the new configuration to apply without reusing ads from the previous selection. If no range is set, you will be asked again the next time the game starts.

Android lets you manage, reset or delete the advertising ID from its privacy settings, depending on the system version. This does not necessarily delete data Google has already processed. To learn about Google’s options, see its policy linked above; for questions about the game, contact us by email.

6. Retention, deletion and security

Progress, settings, counters and their local copies are kept on your device. You can delete them by clearing the game’s storage data from Android settings. This also deletes your progress. Bright Orbit Games does not keep its own copy on a server from which it could be recovered.

If you send us an email or a diagnostics report, we will receive your email address and any information you choose to include. It will be used to handle your request and kept for as long as needed to resolve it and meet applicable obligations. You can request its deletion by writing to the same contact. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

Google applies its own retention periods and deletion mechanisms. Data transmitted by its advertising SDK is encrypted in transit. Local files rely on your device’s protections; no system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Target audience

Maze Escape is intended for teenagers aged 13 and over and for adults, and is not specifically designed for children under 13. This does not mean that all of its users are adults: additional protections for teenagers may apply depending on their country.

For the 13–17 range, restricted, non-personalized ad processing is requested. For 18 and over, personalization depends on consent preferences and the service’s restrictions. If you do not indicate a range or choose under 13, the game does not start its advertising flow. The regular game options and the free retry remain available.

If you are responsible for a minor and have a question about information they shared with Bright Orbit Games, write to our privacy contact.

8. Questions, rights and changes

You can write to us to ask about data we have received directly, to request access, correction or deletion, or to exercise the rights that apply under applicable law. Information processed directly by Google is also subject to its own privacy mechanisms.

If the way the game or its services work changes, we will update this policy and its date. Changes that require additional notice or consent will be communicated as appropriate.
